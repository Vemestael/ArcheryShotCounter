## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Archery Shot Counter automatically counts your shots during practice — no buttons, no phone, just your watch. Built by an archer who got tired of losing count mid-session and wanted real numbers on their training — if that sounds like you, or you just don't want to hand-count anymore, this is for you.

### HOW IT WORKS
The app listens to your watch's wrist accelerometer and detects the sharp recoil that happens when your bowstring releases. When a shot is detected, your watch vibrates and the count goes up — completely hands-free, whether you're shooting compound, Olympic recurve, barebow, or traditional.

### FEATURES
- Automatic shot detection — just draw and shoot
- Adjustable sensitivity (4 presets, or set your own threshold) to match your bow and which wrist you wear the watch on
- Configurable cooldown between shots to avoid false triggers
- Manual +/- adjustment if you ever need to correct the count
- Session history — browse past training sessions with dates, duration, and shot counts
- Per-shot detail view — see the exact time and impact strength of every recorded shot
- "Shots per end" tracking with optional auto-pause between ends
- Export and import your training data, so you're always in control of your own history
- Works with or without Always-On Display — dims the screen automatically to save battery either way
- 12 languages: English, Russian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Turkish, Hindi

### PRIVACY
Archery Shot Counter has no internet permission and never sends any data anywhere. Everything is stored locally on your watch, under your control.

### REQUIREMENTS
A Wear OS 3.0+ smartwatch (Android API 30+) with a wrist accelerometer — standard on all Wear OS watches. Developed and tested on a Samsung Galaxy Watch 6 Classic.
